## Overview

To start a new project with AAMP Development, simply register and create your first workspace.

## Steps

1. Sign up at `https://app.aampdev.com`
2. Login to your dashboard
3. Click "Create New Project"
4. Name your project and select a template
5. Click "Create" to launch your workspace

<Callout kind="info">
Explore templates to speed up initialization!
</Callout>
